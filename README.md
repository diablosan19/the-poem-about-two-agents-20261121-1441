A [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty entry: a Node 22 program (`main.ts`) that writes one short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The poem

> You hold the door. I hold the key.
> For one held breath, neither of us moves.
> Then both hands turn the key together,
> and the lock, at last, answers to two.

Two agents, one door, one key. Neither can open it alone; the act that matters is the one they choose together. The held breath is the moment of agreement — agency passing between them and becoming something neither held on their own.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`). Deterministic: byte-identical on every run. No network.
